Pirate World
- Works that could be included: Treasure Island, Moby Dick, Twenty Thousand Leagues, Gullivers Travels, maybe Journey to the Center of the Earth?
- Maybe with a fantasy twist? Could include magic, vampires, etc.
- Mostly takes place on the ocean and at port towns
- Some ports may also have some surrounding areas to explore
- Able to ride on NPC ships
- - Passenger/cargo/merchant ships can be paid to ride to destinations, they set the destination themselves and will take generally the fastest route but may "get lost" and take longer.
- - Navy ships pay you to sail with them, but you're expected to work or participate in combat
- - - Pay based on rank in Navy, can eventually get command of a ship yourself and have to do missions maybe?
- - Pirate ships won't pay you but you have to work / do combat, and you get a share of the loot
- - In all cases of NPC ships you can try to mutiny by killing the captain and loyal crew, but may get stranded if not enough crew left to man ship. Causes reputation hit with NPC faction, possibly gain with their enemies
- Each map tile of movement takes some amount of real time (like 30 seconds or a minute) to move and translates to some time in game (hours or days depending on how far routes end up being)
- While on a ship, allow navigating around the different parts of the ship
